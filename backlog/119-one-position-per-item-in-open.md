# 119 One position per item in Open and Waiting

As the user, I want a waiting item to show the same position everywhere, so that two different numbers for one item never confuse me.

Category: ux

## What

Items in the plan's Waiting group also appear in Open, and each group numbers its own rows, so one item shows two different positions. Decide one rule (for example: Waiting shows the item's Open position, or no position at all) and apply it, with tests.

## Why

Raised by the job 344 ux review (nit 2): the spec accepted two positions per waiting item, but it can be confusing when you talk about an item's place.
