# 021 Quick capture

As the user, I want to jot down an idea on the phone and have it land in a project's backlog as a draft, so that I don't lose ideas while I'm away from the Mac.

## What

A Capture action takes a project and a short free text (typed or dictated with the phone's keyboard) and adds a `[?]` item with the next number: a story file with the text under `## What`, `## Why` saying it still needs writing, and a Notes line `Draft from ControlRoom`. The next factory session (`/factory groom` or a person) rewrites it into a proper story before it is approved. Same write checks as #4. Full path, high risk (writes).

## Why

Ideas come up away from the desk, and writing a full story on a phone is too slow. A draft that waits as `[?]` keeps the idea without letting a half-written item run.
