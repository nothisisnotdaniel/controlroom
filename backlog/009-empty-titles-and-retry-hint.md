# 009 Empty titles and retry hint

As the user, I want a backlog line with an empty title to show a placeholder, and the read error to tell me to reload, so that odd lines and errors don't leave me guessing.

## What

A backlog line with an empty title shows a fixed placeholder, and the "can't be read" line tells the user to reload to try again.

## Why

An empty bold title looks like a rendering bug, and a red "can't be read" line with no next step leaves me stuck. The UX reviewer of job 125 noted both.
