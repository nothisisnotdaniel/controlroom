# 108 Position numbers in backlog groups

As the user, I want a position number in front of every backlog item, so that I can see and talk about its place in the order at a glance.

## What

Show the item's 1-based position within its group (1, 2, 3 … in file order) in front of every line in every group, as a visually distinct number that is not the item number (#N stays as it is). In the Open group it matches the order the factory takes items. Screen readers hear it as "position N".

## Why

The order of the Open group is the priority, but today nothing shows where an item stands without counting lines.
