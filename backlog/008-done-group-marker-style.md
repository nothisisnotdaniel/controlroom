# 008 Done-group marker style

As the user, I want the marker of the collapsed "done" group to fit the app's look, so that the backlogs view looks finished in light and dark mode.

## What

Give the disclosure marker of the collapsed "done" group in the backlogs view a style that fits the app in light and dark mode.

## Why

The browser's default disclosure marker works, but it stands out against the app's own styling. The UI reviewer of job 125 noted it as a nit.
